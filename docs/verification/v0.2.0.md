## Actual model-output A/B evaluation

Six synthetic cases, three fresh generation threads per arm, six tasks per thread: **18 old and 18 new outputs**. Initial generation found API assumptions and cross-task naming; the final revised new skill generated another 18 outputs. All initial artifacts are retained rather than replaced. Two fresh model judges independently scored the final shuffled, version-hidden 36-sample set. No model or sampling overrides were made; the precise server build and sampling parameters are not exposed. Tasks within one thread are correlated. The old arm remains the original baseline, including its defects.

| Task group | Old /100 | New /100 | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Knowledge: cases 1–3 | 99.17 | 98.75 | −0.42 |
| Technical and boundaries: cases 4–6 | 90.63 | 99.17 | +8.54 |

These are weighted model-judge averages, not human performance measurements. Final judges flagged three old API samples for invented contract semantics or encoding; no final new sample received a hard-failure flag. The initial judges flagged only one old hard failure, illustrating calibration variability. The final new set still has minor repetition, an occasional source-by-source structure, and literal-command preservation deductions. Adding `-i` to curl preserves its purpose but some judges penalized replacing the exact supplied spelling.

The advisory threshold in the supplied candidate plan was a knowledge-task gain of at least 8 points, technical regression at most 3 points, and no new hard failures. **The knowledge gain threshold was not met.** The near-perfect baseline leaves little room on these simple cases. Do not claim general superiority or change the threshold retroactively. A more discriminating, preregistered held-out benchmark and human reading study remain future work.

A separate model reader answered comprehension and task-reconstruction questions using only individual outputs. These answers are raw answerability evidence, not timed reading, human comprehension, or proof of task execution. No fictitious Lantern service was run. Output length and per-dimension scores are in the machine-readable summary; length is not a measure of information density by itself.
